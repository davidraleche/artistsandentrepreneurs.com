Artists & Entrepreneurs Latest News

Artists & Entrepreneurs (“A&E”) has been established to serve as a resource for foreign designers, artists, entrepreneurs and others who want to live and work in the United States.

In 2025, A&E had 38 O-1 approvals for a range of professions, including photographers, hair & make-up artists, journalists, musicians, dancers, actresses, lighting designers, fashion/textile designers, UX/product designers, architectural designers, curators, and AI consultants.

Artists & Entrepreneurs serves as a visa sponsor for those who need an agent-petitioner in the United States for O or P visa purposes. Many O and P visa applicants are self-employed and work as independent contractors. Hence, they do not have full-time employers to sponsor them.

The O-1 agent scenario is ideal for designers, artists, and others who wish to work on a self-employed basis for multiple employers on short-term contracts or engagements. In this case, A&E, as agent, will sign the O-1 visa application and will serve as the representative, for immigration purposes, of both the O-1 visa applicant and the various employers that the applicant will be working for. A schedule of engagements must be provided for the O-1 period requested.

Another option for the agent- based O-1 is where the O-1 applicant has a full-time overseas employer who is sending them to the US and provides an employment contract and evidence of US-based work. Here, A&E can serve as US agent for the overseas employer.

A&E can also facilitate where the O-1 visa applicant has a full-time US employer but that employer does not wish to sign any immigration-related paperwork. In this case, A&E can serve as agent for the employer and sign the paperwork if the employer authorizes A&E to do so.

Note: A&E can only serve as agent-petitioner where the O-1 visa applicant has qualified legal counsel.
